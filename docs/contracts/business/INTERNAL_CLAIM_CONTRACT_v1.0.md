# Internal Claim Contract v1.0

Internal Claim owns customer complaint, quality, schedule, contract, cost, incident, corrective action and recurrence-prevention workflow for company operations.

It is not an external Claim Service case. Conversion requires an explicit command, approval and a new Claim Service ID. canonical projectId is the only Project linkage.

Permissions, category, timeline, action, resolution, retention and reporting are Internal Claim scoped. State is RECEIVED, TRIAGE, INVESTIGATING, ACTION_REQUIRED, RESOLUTION_PROPOSED, RESOLVED, CLOSED, REOPENED or CANCELLED.

# Claim Service Evidence and Report Contract v1.0

External Claim Service is a separate professional-service bounded context for construction disputes, contract issues, loss/cost analysis, court/arbitration support, evidence, timeline and reports.

Evidence preserves immutable original, checksum, version, source, collectedBy, collectedAt, access classification, Legal Hold and audit. Legal Hold approval requires the Claim Center owner plus Legal/Compliance or designated executive approver; unilateral release is forbidden.

Recording requires consent or approved legal basis. Retention is returned by legal/security policy capability. Original audio and STT transcript are separate versioned artifacts with human review.

Reports follow DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → ISSUED, with SUPERSEDED/WITHDRAWN branches. AI cannot approve or issue a legal report.
